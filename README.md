# Patter — Language Learning

**Language learning that feels like texting a friend.**

Chat with AI personas who stay in character and reply only in your target language. Corrections appear inline. No drills, no account, no subscription.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/patter-language-learning/id6762643113)

---

## What it is

Patter is a serverless iOS app. You create contacts — AI personas with a name, backstory, and tone — and text them. They reply only in the language you're learning. When you slip, a quiet correction appears beneath your message showing what went wrong and why.

Everything runs on your device. No developer backend. No account required.

## Features

- **Inline corrections** — wrong → right, with a one-line reason. Tap to dismiss.
- **Tap-to-translate** — tap any AI message to see it in your language.
- **CEFR levels A1–C1** — switch level mid-conversation; the persona adapts without resetting.
- **Personas** — each contact has a name, emoji, backstory, tone, and interests. They stay in character.
- **Streak tracking** — daily message goals and consecutive-day streaks, via local notifications.
- **Serverless / BYOK** — bring your own API key (Google AI Studio, OpenAI, Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible provider). Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain and never leaves your device.

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later
- An API key from a supported provider: Google AI Studio, OpenAI, or Anthropic

## Privacy

Conversations are stored locally via SwiftData. API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. No data is routed through a developer-owned server. See the full [Privacy Policy](https://vilaverdeapps.github.io/patter/PRIVACY.html).
